# Changelog

## v13.0 · Afterlight

- Reworked five districts with new cover, alternate routes, landmarks, atmospheric skies, and concrete surface detail.
- Added twelve new first-person blaster models with reload movement, recoil, and layered firing sounds.
- Added depth-tested projectile trails, sparks, overload debris, and supply pulses.
- Added campaign wave events, shop forecasts, and timed signal caches. Corrected radar heading.

## v12.0 visual update

- Opened up the reflex sight lens so the center dot stays visible against the scene.
- Surfaced all five arenas with light concrete slabs and subtle joints.
- Adapted seven Quaternius robot meshes for eight enemy roles, with role colors and distance-based detail.

## v12.0 — Classic Optics

- Replaced bright cyan precision sight with a traditional circular FPS scope.
- Added dark matte exterior, subtle lens vignette, fine crosshair and hash marks.
- Replaced angular holographic overlay for other blasters with a centered red-dot sight and raised weapon.
- Added smooth frame-rate-independent ADS FOV transition.
- Preserved v11 world depth rendering and all previous gameplay features.
